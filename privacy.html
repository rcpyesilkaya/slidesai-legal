Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Slides AI iOS app ("the app", "we") handles, where it goes and the choices you have. We designed the app to keep your content on your device whenever possible.

1. Content you create stays on your device

Your presentations, notes, flashcards, scanned pages, imported files and audio recordings are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not run a server that stores your documents. Exported files are saved only where you choose to save or share them.

2. AI processing

When you use an AI feature, the text needed for that request is processed in one of these ways. The app shows which one applies before you send.

  • Apple Intelligence (on device): on supported iPhones the request is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us.
  • Google Gemini via Firebase: if Apple Intelligence is not available, or you select Gemini in settings, the text of your request (for example your topic, instructions, or the text extracted from a document, scan or transcript) is sent over an encrypted connection to Google's Gemini API through Google Firebase to generate the result. It is used to produce the response and is handled under Firebase and Gemini API terms. Original images, audio and files are not uploaded; only the extracted text is sent.
  • Basic mode: simple text rules run entirely on your iPhone, with no AI service involved.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in content you send to a cloud AI service.

3. Camera, microphone, speech recognition and photos

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a document. Text is recognized on your device.
  • Microphone: used only after you tap Record. Recordings are stored on your device.
  • Speech recognition: turns your recordings into text, on device whenever your iPhone supports it. Otherwise Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Photo library (add only): used only to save exported slide images when you ask.

4. Account and identifiers

The app does not require you to create an account with a password. The name and optional email you enter in onboarding are stored on your device (including a Keychain backup so they survive a reinstall) and are not sent to us.

To protect the AI service from abuse and keep your subscription across reinstalls, the app signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication, which creates a random user ID that is not linked to your name or email. Firebase App Check (Apple App Attest) is used to verify that requests come from the genuine app.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status; RevenueCat receives the anonymous user ID above and purchase information from Apple (such as product, purchase date and renewal status). See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • We do not build profiles from your documents.

7. Third-party service providers

  • Apple (App Store, StoreKit, Apple Intelligence, speech recognition)
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, App Check, AI Logic) and Google Gemini API
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)

8. Data retention and deletion

Content on your device stays until you delete it or delete the app. In the app, Profile → Delete Account & All Data removes your local documents and profile, deletes the anonymous Firebase user and logs out of RevenueCat. Purchase records kept by Apple and RevenueCat for billing and legal reasons are governed by their policies. You can also email us to request deletion of any data associated with you.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. Because we store very little, most of your data is under your control on your device. Contact us to exercise your rights.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app or on this page.

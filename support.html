Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Slides AI and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my content processed?

On supported iPhones, with Apple Intelligence on your device. Otherwise the request text is sent securely to Google Gemini. The app shows which engine is used, and you can change it in Profile → AI Engine. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account & All Data.
